    cosmetic: `DROP POLICY IF EXISTS policy_scope_isolation ON thoughts`; `ALTER TABLE thoughts NO

    FROM thoughts WHERE id = ANY(${testIds})`. Do NOT delete before disabling RLS: a `DELETE` issued

    statement, and the `USING` clause filters `DELETE` exactly as it filters `SELECT` — an unscoped
